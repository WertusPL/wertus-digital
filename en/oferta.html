What do we mean by a revision round?

One revision round means a single, consolidated list of changes sent after we present a given stage of the project. Revisions cover the previously agreed scope and the presented concept.

Changing an approved direction, adding new pages or functionality, or a significant rebuild of the project counts as a change of scope and is quoted separately.
