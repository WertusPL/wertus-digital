Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

1. Data controller

The controller of personal data submitted through wertusdigital.pl is Wertus Digital, based in Białystok, Poland. For any matters related to personal data, contact us at kontakt@wertusdigital.pl or by phone: +48 516 950 801.

2. What data we process

We only process data you provide voluntarily through the contact form: your name, e-mail address and the content of your message. If you contact us directly by e-mail or phone, we process the data contained in that correspondence.

3. Purpose and legal basis

We use the data from the form to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and handle further correspondence about potential cooperation. The legal basis is taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and the controller's legitimate interest in handling correspondence (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. How long we keep the data

We keep the data for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and discuss cooperation. If no cooperation follows, the correspondence is deleted after the contact ends, at the latest after the period needed to establish or defend possible claims.

5. Who we share the data with

Messages sent through the contact form are handled by Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA), which delivers them to our e-mail inbox. The website is hosted on Vercel (Vercel Inc., USA). These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and safeguards applicable to data transfers outside the EEA (including standard contractual clauses). We do not sell or share your data with other parties for marketing purposes.

6. Cookies and analytics

wertusdigital.pl does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics and marketing tools. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (e.g. IP address, request time) to ensure security and proper operation of the website.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format and object to its processing. To exercise these rights, write to kontakt@wertusdigital.pl. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland).

8. Voluntary provision of data

Providing your data is voluntary, but necessary for us to reply to an enquiry sent through the contact form.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated, for example when the services we use change. The current version is always available at wertusdigital.pl/privacy-policy.
